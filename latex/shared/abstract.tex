We study generalized Bayesian inference over complete deterministic policies
while preserving the ordinary expected-return objective of a stochastic Markov
decision process.  The target is a Gibbs measure whose log density is
proportional to a policy's expected return.  It is therefore different from a
trajectory target that averages exponentiated realized returns, which is
risk-sensitive even when the intended control objective is not.  Our primary
algorithm is replica-exchange Metropolis--Hastings in policy space.  A learned
PPO policy supplies full-support single-state proposals, but an exact Hastings
correction prevents the guide from changing the invariant distribution.  When
the transition model is available, dynamic programming evaluates the exact
target; with simulator-only access, an immutable bank of common random-number
tapes defines a transparent sample-average approximation.  Direct variational
and replicated sequential Monte Carlo estimators provide complementary
ablations.  Evaluation samples one complete policy and commits to it for the
episode, and compares against precisely specified REINFORCE, PPO-Clip, discrete
SAC, categorical cross-entropy search, and Double Q-learning baselines under
matched simulator access and compute accounting in a separate information
stratum.  Exact finite-policy gates precede
larger stochastic GridWorld experiments.  Confirmatory empirical results are
not yet claimed in this draft; medium-scale mixing and cost gates remain in
progress.
